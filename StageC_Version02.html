    Priority, highest first: T1 interactive prompts (safety alerts before calls), T2 media feedback, T3 main menu, T4 user and system content. A higher tier shows first and lower ones wait in line, then appear in order when it clears. The menu and the page content are not queued: a prompt closes the menu, and the page stays underneath.

    Try it: open a prompt, then press Volume overlay and Locked Settings (turns vehicleIsMoving on and opens Settings). Both wait until you answer the prompt. Directions and trip information turn guidanceIsActive on and open the Navigation page; both show on that page only. The guidance banner stays up while guidance is active, and trip information is also toggled by Center on that page. Alert prompts stay pressed while their condition is active, even after the rider dismisses them. With vehicleIsMoving on, Settings stays in the menu. The "Settings are hidden" pill stays on the Settings page while the vehicle moves and the menu is closed, never over the menu.
