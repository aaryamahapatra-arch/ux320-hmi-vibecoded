Overlay queue

    Layers from the top: 5 prompts, 4 media feedback, 3 main menu, 2 page. They can all be on screen at once and the highest layer captures the D-pad. Only prompts compete with each other: they share one stack, the highest priority is on screen (safety alerts before calls, same priority keeps arrival order) and the rest are queued, then appear in order as each one closes. While a prompt is on screen the menu and feedback timeouts are paused; they restart when the last prompt closes. With more than one overlay open, the volume feedback shrinks to a toast at the top.

    Rubric demos. 1) Prompt conflict: press "Call + low fuel at once" (low fuel is on screen, the call is queued) or "Low tyre + side stand at once" (same priority: the first one stays on screen), and read the state readout. 2) Multi-overlay conflict: press "Menu + volume + prompt at once", or open the menu with Page, press a volume key, then open a prompt. Directions and trip information turn guidanceIsActive on and open the Navigation page. Locked Settings turns vehicleIsMoving on and opens Settings; the "Settings are hidden" pill stays on that page while the menu is closed and no prompt or volume overlay is up. Alert prompts stay pressed while their condition is active, even after the rider dismisses them.

    Text field test hook: in the browser console, hmiSet("odometer", "longest") forces the longest string of a field and brings it on screen, hmiCheck("odometer") reports clipping or wrapping, hmiCheckAll() runs every field, hmiReset() clears it. hmiFields() lists the fields with their format, range, step, no-data text and longest string.
